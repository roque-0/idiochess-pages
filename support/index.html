Contact

For help, feedback, or feature requests, email hello@roquejean.com.

Include your Idiochess username, app version, iOS version, device model, and a description of what happened when relevant. Do not include passwords, Apple authorization details, authentication codes, notification tokens, or private invitation links.

Sign in with Apple

Version 1.0 uses Sign in with Apple. Confirm that your device has an active internet connection, is signed in to an Apple Account, and allows Idiochess to use Sign in with Apple. If authorization is cancelled, reopen Idiochess and try again.

Army Setup

Drag pieces from the tray onto permitted starting squares. Every army requires exactly one King and must stay within the displayed budget. In Strict mode, squares marked with an X are unavailable. The Start Game action becomes available when the setup is valid.

Matchmaking and Invitations

Start Game searches for an eligible opponent. If none is available, your completed setup is saved as a waiting challenge. This is expected; you may cancel it from Challenges or play an Idiobot while waiting.

Friend invitation links expire after seven days and must be opened by someone other than the sender. After installing Idiochess, open the original invitation link again to accept the challenge.

Notifications

Idiochess uses notifications for challenge and game events. They are optional and can be managed in iOS Settings under Notifications → Idiochess. Disabling notifications does not prevent gameplay.

Blocking and Reporting

Open another player’s profile and use its kebab menu to block or report that player. Manage existing blocks under your profile’s kebab menu → Settings → Blocked Players.

Delete Your Account

In Idiochess, tap your avatar on Home, open the kebab menu on your profile, select Delete Account, and confirm. Apple may request fresh authorization. Deletion removes your sign-in identity and active player data; completed opponent records may remain in anonymized form to preserve legitimate game history and ratings.

Account deletion is permanent. If you cannot access the in-app deletion control, contact support at the email address above.
