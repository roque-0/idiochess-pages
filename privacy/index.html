Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Idiochess collects, uses, discloses, and retains information when you use the Idiochess mobile application, its invitation and verification pages, and related services. By using Idiochess, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to provide accounts, games, and community features:

  • Account information. Your email address and authentication identifiers. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide an email address and name according to the choices you make with Apple. Authentication credentials are processed by our authentication provider; Idiochess does not display your email publicly.
  • Profile information. Your username, selected Idiochess avatar, country if provided, ratings, rankings, game statistics, join date, and profile settings. Version 1.0 does not allow custom photo uploads.
  • Gameplay information. Challenges, matchmaking preferences, game setups, moves, results, ratings, game history, Arcana progress, earned in-game fragments, and invitation status.
  • Safety information. Player blocks, reports, report reasons, the reported username at the time of a report, and moderation status. Reports and block relationships are not displayed publicly.
  • Notification and technical information. A device push-notification token if you enable notifications, along with technical request, security, and diagnostic information generated by the services that operate Idiochess, such as IP address, timestamps, device or app information, and error records.

Idiochess does not include third-party advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, and support Sign in with Apple.
  • Operate games, matchmaking, invitations, ratings, rankings, and game history.
  • Display the profile and competitive information you choose to associate with your account.
  • Send challenge, game, account, and service notifications when enabled.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate reports, enforce blocks, protect game integrity, and secure the service.
  • Diagnose failures, maintain reliability, and improve Idiochess.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce the Terms of Service.

4. Information Visible to Other Players

Idiochess is a multiplayer game. Your username, selected avatar, country flag if provided, ratings, rankings, game statistics, join date, public challenge information, and portions of your game history may be visible to other players. Completed game records may identify both participants so that opponents can review legitimate results and replays.

Blocking changes how the blocked player is presented to you and prevents supported future interactions. It does not erase completed games or change ratings. Reports remain private and do not reveal the reporting player to the reported player.

5. Service Providers and Disclosure

We use a limited number of providers to operate Idiochess:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and server functions.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and distributes the iOS application.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications.
  • GitHub Pages hosts invitation, email-verification, and legal-information pages.

These providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect players or the service, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a business transfer. We do not permit service providers to use Idiochess data for their own advertising.

6. Reasons for Processing

Depending on where you live, we process information because it is necessary to provide the service you request, because we have legitimate interests in securing and improving Idiochess, because you have given consent, or because processing is required by law. You may withdraw consent where applicable, but some features may then be unavailable.

7. Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and comply with obligations relating to Idiochess. Challenge invitation links are designed to expire after seven days.

You can delete your account from the self-profile menu in the app. Deletion removes your authentication account and active profile information. Completed game records may be retained in anonymized form so opponents keep an accurate history, rating record, and replay. Safety and moderation records may also be retained when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, prevent repeated violations, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Limited information may remain temporarily in provider backups and security logs before routine deletion.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can edit supported profile fields, manage blocked players, disable notifications in your device settings, and delete your account in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or an objection to certain processing. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including authenticated server operations and secure device storage for session credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so you should use a unique password and protect access to your device and account.

10. Children

Idiochess is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information in violation of this policy, contact us through the support channel below so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

11. International Processing

Idiochess and its providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Idiochess changes. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice when required. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use, subject to rights provided by applicable law.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact information on the Idiochess Support page. Please do not include passwords, authentication codes, or invitation tokens in a support request.
